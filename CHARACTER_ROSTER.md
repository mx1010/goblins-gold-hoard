- **Skrit Quickpurse (Rogue, Goblin)** — stealth coding and infiltration tasks
- **Miri Emberquill (Wizard, Gnome)** — spell logic, synthesis, and arcane tooling

- **Crow-Eye Rill** — raven master coordinating messenger routes

## Flavor Notes

- Adventurers gather in torch-lit chambers, not cubicles.
- Reports are archived in the Goblin Grimoire.
- Completed quests are celebrated with treasure tallies, not office metrics.
