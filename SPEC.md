# Goblin's Gold Hoard Spec

## Scope

This specification defines a full thematic reskin from office simulation to dungeon-crawler fantasy without changing functional harness behavior.

## Required Theme Transformations

- "Office" references become **Warren** references.
- "Munder Difflin" references become **Goblin's Gold Hoard**.
- "Michael" or GOD-overseer framing becomes **Gruk**.
- Task language shifts from paper workflows to raids, quests, and heists.

## UI and Content Requirements

- Dungeon palette: `#2D5016`, `#FFD700`, `#4B0082`, `#708090`
- Scroll-like display wording for command and log surfaces
- Messenger raven/courier framing for route-and-deliver systems
- Spell compendium framing for skills catalog content

## Agent Gallery (Hires)

- **Rogue** — stealthy coder
- **Wizard** — spell-casting AI specialist
- **Paladin** — defender and security reviewer
- **Bard** — documentation and storytelling writer
- **Ranger** — scout and explorer

## Flavor Example

> "Gruk commands the Warren: recover the moonstone idol before dawn.  
> The ranger scouts, the rogue infiltrates, and the wizard deciphers the vault sigils while the bard records the raid on fresh scribe scrolls."
